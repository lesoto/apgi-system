# ISO 27001 Compliance Documentation

## Overview

This document outlines the APGI System's implementation of ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Information Security Management System (ISMS) requirements.

## Information Security Policy

### Policy Statement

The APGI System is committed to maintaining the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of all information assets under our control. We implement an Information Security Management System (ISMS) compliant with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 to protect sensitive research data, clinical information, and intellectual property.

### Scope

This ISMS applies to:

- All APGI System software components and infrastructure
- Data processing and storage systems
- Development and deployment environments
- Third-party service integrations
- Personnel with access to system resources

### Information Security Objectives

1. **Confidentiality**: Protect sensitive data from unauthorized access
2. **Integrity**: Ensure data accuracy and completeness
3. **Availability**: Maintain system uptime and service continuity
4. **Compliance**: Meet regulatory requirements (GDPR, HIPAA, SOC 2)

## Risk Management Process

### Risk Assessment Methodology

1. **Asset Identification**: Catalog all information assets
2. **Threat Analysis**: Identify potential threats and vulnerabilities
3. **Risk Evaluation**: Assess likelihood and impact using risk matrix
4. **Risk Treatment**: Select appropriate treatment (avoid, mitigate, transfer, accept)
5. **Risk Monitoring**: Continuous monitoring and review

### Risk Register

| Risk ID | Asset | Threat | Likelihood | Impact | Risk Level | Treatment | Owner |

| R001 | Clinical Data | Unauthorized Access | Medium | High | High | Mitigate | Security Team |
| R002 | API Endpoints | DDoS Attack | Low | High | Medium | Mitigate | DevOps |
| R003 | Database | Data Loss | Low | Critical | High | Mitigate | DBA |
| R004 | Source Code | IP Theft | Low | Medium | Low | Accept | Legal |

## Incident Management

### Incident Response Plan

1. **Detection**: Automated monitoring and user reports
2. **Classification**: Severity levels (Critical, High, Medium, Low)
3. **Response**: Incident response team activation
4. **Containment**: Immediate actions to limit impact
5. **Eradication**: Root cause analysis and remediation
6. **Recovery**: System restoration and validation
7. **Post-Incident**: Lessons learned and process improvement

### Incident Categories

- **Security Incidents**: Unauthorized access, data breaches
- **Availability Incidents**: Service outages, performance degradation
- **Integrity Incidents**: Data corruption, unauthorized modifications
- **Compliance Incidents**: Policy violations, regulatory non-compliance

## Business Continuity

### Continuity Planning

- **RTO (Recovery Time Objective)**: 4 hours for critical systems
- **RPO (Recovery Point Objective)**: 15 minutes for data loss
- **Backup Strategy**: Daily full backups, hourly incremental backups
- **Disaster Recovery**: Multi-region deployment, failover procedures

### Testing

- **DR Tests**: Quarterly full disaster recovery tests
- **Backup Restoration**: Monthly verification
- **Continuity Exercises**: Annual tabletop exercises

## Compliance Monitoring

### Internal Audits

- **Frequency**: Semi-annual
- **Scope**: All ISMS controls
- **Method**: Document review, technical testing, interviews
- **Reporting**: Executive summary with findings and recommendations

### Management Review

- **Frequency**: Quarterly
- **Participants**: Executive team, security team, IT leadership
- **Agenda**: ISMS performance, risk status, audit findings, improvement opportunities
- **Output**: Management review minutes with action items

### Continuous Improvement

- **PDCA Cycle**: Plan-Do-Check-Act approach
- **KPIs**: Security incidents, audit findings, training completion
- **Feedback**: User surveys, stakeholder reviews
- **Updates**: Annual ISMS review and updates
